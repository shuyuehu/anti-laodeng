# Red Flags

Use this file to detect when workplace communication is drifting into 老登 mode.

## 1. Seniority Over Evidence

Common signals:
- “我做这行二十年了”
- “你还年轻，你不懂”
- “先别解释，按我说的做”

Why it is risky:
- It ends discussion with rank or age instead of facts.
- It teaches people that being correct matters less than flattering power.

Safer direction:
- Anchor the decision in current constraints, data, or explicit ownership.

## 2. Disagreement Turned Into an Attitude Problem

Common signals:
- “你这是抬杠”
- “你先统一思想”
- “你是不是态度有问题”

Why it is risky:
- It makes dissent feel unsafe.
- It teaches teams to hide risk and say what leaders want to hear.

Safer direction:
- Separate disagreement from disrespect.
- State whether the moment is for discussion, recommendation, or execution.

## 3. Vague Pressure Without Support

Common signals:
- “我只看结果”
- “办法你自己想”
- “别给我理由”

Why it is risky:
- The receiver gets pressure without scope, priority, resources, or tradeoffs.
- It creates blame-friendly ambiguity.

Safer direction:
- Clarify target, owner, deadline, decision rights, and what can move if this becomes top priority.

## 4. Abstract Character Judgment

Common signals:
- “你不成熟”
- “你没格局”
- “你站位不够”
- “你抗压不行”

Why it is risky:
- It attacks identity instead of work.
- It leaves the other person unable to improve because the standard stays fuzzy.

Safer direction:
- Replace vibe words with observable examples and a specific expectation.

## 5. Moralizing and Gratitude Pressure

Common signals:
- “我都是为你好”
- “公司给了你平台，你要懂感恩”
- “我骂你是在培养你”

Why it is risky:
- It reframes power and workload as moral debt.
- It blocks legitimate pushback by making disagreement feel ungrateful.

Safer direction:
- Say the business reason, the standard, and the support plan plainly.

## 6. Boundary Erosion Through Fake Intimacy

Common signals:
- “团队就是一家人”
- “我把你当自己人才这么说”
- “别老讲边界感”

Why it is risky:
- It uses closeness language to ask for extra compliance.
- It often blurs work scope with emotional obligation.

Safer direction:
- Keep the request inside role, scope, timeline, and reciprocity.

## 7. Care Used as Control

Common signals:
- “稳定最重要，别折腾”
- “你以后会感谢我的”
- “我替你决定更好”

Why it is risky:
- It substitutes the leader's risk preference for the other person's agency.
- It sounds protective but functions as control.

Safer direction:
- Offer advice as one perspective and leave room for the other person's choice.

## 8. Public Shame, Private Ambiguity

Common signals:
- Calling out people in meetings without prior private feedback
- Public sarcasm, eye-rolling, or comparison
- Private instructions that become public blame later

Why it is risky:
- It raises fear, not clarity.
- It rewards politics and memory battles over written alignment.

Safer direction:
- Correct in private when possible and document decisions in writing when they matter.
