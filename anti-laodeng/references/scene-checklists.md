# Scene Checklists

Use the checklist that matches the situation. Ask these questions before approving the user's draft or advice.

## Meeting Or Group Discussion

Check:
- Did the speaker separate discussion time from decision time?
- Did they leave room for disagreement without calling it attitude?
- Did they explain the current constraint instead of leaning on seniority?
- Did they clarify who decides and by when?

Good outcome:
- People know whether they are being asked to debate, recommend, or execute.

## Feedback Or Mentoring

Check:
- Is the feedback about work, not personality?
- Is there at least one observable example?
- Is the next expected behavior explicit?
- Did the speaker offer support, examples, or a review point?

Good outcome:
- The receiver knows what to improve and how success will be judged.

## Performance Review

Check:
- Are claims supported by examples, metrics, or repeated patterns?
- Are vague words like “mature,” “stable,” or “strategic” translated into evidence?
- Does the review avoid age-coded, gender-coded, or relationship-coded language?
- Are consequences and growth paths concrete?

Good outcome:
- The review reads as fair, specific, and actionable rather than vibe-based.

## Assignment Or Escalation

Check:
- Is the priority explicit?
- Is the owner explicit?
- Is the deadline explicit?
- Are tradeoffs explicit?
- Is the manager owning the decision instead of pushing all risk downward?

Good outcome:
- The task is demanding but not blame-friendly.

## One-On-One Or Informal Chat

Check:
- Is the speaker giving advice or quietly taking control?
- Are they crossing into personal life without invitation?
- Are they using “for your own good” language to close discussion?
- Are they turning closeness into obligation?

Good outcome:
- The message stays respectful of agency and boundaries.

## Repair After A Bad Interaction

Check:
- Did the speaker acknowledge impact without overdramatizing intent?
- Did they restate the real expectation clearly?
- Did they move the correction out of public shame and into a usable next step?
- Did they summarize the agreed next move in writing if the issue mattered?

Repair template:
- “刚才我的表达方式有压人和不清楚的地方，我重新说一次。我要解决的是 X，不是给你贴标签。接下来请你在 Y 时间前完成 Z，如果卡住了提前告诉我。”
